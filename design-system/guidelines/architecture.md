## 02 — Recommendation

**Model: Branded house.** RWC has one mission, limited resources, overlapping audiences, and one name with existing recognition. Independent sub-brands would dilute trust and multiply maintenance. Every event, resource, sponsor program, and product should visibly build Red White and Clix equity.

## 03 — Hierarchy

```text
Red White and Clix
├── RWC Annual Event — flagship, year appended
│   ├── 300 Modern Championship — descriptive format
│   ├── Team Sealed — descriptive format
│   ├── Battle Royale — descriptive format
│   └── Hot Clix — named activation; confirm safety and fit annually
├── RWC Veteran Resource Guide — evergreen resource
├── RWC Partners — sponsors and beneficiaries
├── RWC Shop — merchandise and game accessories
└── Future event — RWC + ownable descriptor, after strategy/economics approval
```

Parent positioning transfers to every branch. Event formats may use icons or accent colors but not separate logos. Beneficiary organizations retain their own identities and should be labeled as partners, never folded into RWC.

## 04 — Naming system

- Parent display name: **Red White and Clix**. Use **RWC** only after the full name appears.
- Flagship: **Red White and Clix 2026** or **RWC 2026**, not competing variants such as RWC ’26 in formal logistics.
- Formats: established game-format names remain descriptive.
- Owned programs: **RWC + plain descriptor**—RWC Veteran Resource Guide, RWC Partners, RWC Shop.
- Future events: prefer **RWC + evocative but understandable descriptor**; validate trademark, domain, and social needs before launch.
- Avoid military operation codenames, rank/tier language for donors, acronyms that require explanation, and unsupported “national” labels.

Decision tree: same mission and audience → parent brand; different format → descriptive child; separate beneficiary → partner endorsement; fundamentally different mission/audience with independent funding → revisit endorsed model.

## 05 — Visual system

One master logo and palette. Use the full logo on parent materials; use a formally designed simplified RWC icon only after approval. Events share Archivo Black/Space Grotesk, documentary photography, and layout rules. Differentiate formats through labels, iconography, and limited accents—not new wordmarks.

Before a launch: confirm fit in the hierarchy, name convention, audience, economics, beneficiary, visual extension, URL, and owner for maintenance. Review architecture after each annual event and before any new standalone event or program.
