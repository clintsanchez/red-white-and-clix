# Red White and Clix — messaging system

## Message spine

**Core message:** Tabletop play creates a place to belong and a practical way to stand with veterans.

**Value proposition:** Red White and Clix brings players together for well-run tabletop events where competition, camaraderie and veteran support share the same table.

**Recommended campaign line:** **Play Together. Stand With Veterans.**

The current line, **One Community. One Mission.**, remains usable as an organizational sign-off. Client approval is required before replacing it.

## Message pillars

1. **A seat at the table.** Welcoming events give experienced players and newcomers a clear way to participate.
2. **Competition with camaraderie.** Strong formats and fair play make the event worth attending.
3. **Purpose you can understand.** RWC names its supported organizations and reports confirmed outcomes without exaggeration.
4. **Veteran-founded and locally rooted.** The mission comes from lived service and grows through Midwest relationships.

## Proof hierarchy

Use, in order: verified event listings and venue details; authentic event photographs; IRS nonprofit record; confirmed beneficiary agreements and impact reports; approved testimonials; sponsor permissions. Never use registration-platform counts as paid attendance without reconciliation.

## Audience messages

- **Competitive players:** “A serious HeroClix weekend with clear formats, strong competition and a mission bigger than the standings.”
- **Veterans and families:** “A welcoming table built around camaraderie, respect and participation.”
- **Sponsors:** “Put your support into a visible community event with defined benefits and documented outcomes.”
- **Newcomers:** “You don’t need to know everyone—or everything—to take a seat.”

## Calls to action

Primary: **Register for an event.** Secondary: **Donate**, **Become a sponsor**, **Volunteer**, **Shop in support**. Each page and post should select one primary action.

## Ready-to-use copy

**25 words:** Veteran-founded tabletop events where players compete, connect and support veteran-focused causes—together, at the same table.

**50 words:** Red White and Clix brings gamers, veterans, families and supporters together through organized tabletop events. Players get a welcoming, competitive experience. Partners get a practical way to participate in a veteran-focused mission rooted in Lafayette, Indiana, and built for the wider Midwest community.

**Homepage hero:** “Play Together. Stand With Veterans.” Supporting copy: “Join a welcoming HeroClix community for purposeful competition, real camaraderie and a clear way to support veteran-focused work.” CTA: “Register for the next event.”
