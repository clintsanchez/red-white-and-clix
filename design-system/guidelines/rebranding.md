# Red White and Clix — brand refresh roadmap

## Recommendation

Run a focused **brand refresh and operational alignment**, not a full rebrand. Keep the name, recognizable logo idea, red/white/blue equity, founder-led mission and tabletop focus. Change the system around them so the organization looks as credible and organized as the event it promises.

## Refresh scope

- Redraw the logo as clean vector masters and add horizontal, one-color and favicon variants.
- Adopt accessible UI colors while preserving original logo colors.
- Standardize Archivo Black and Space Grotesk roles.
- Use “Play Together. Stand With Veterans.” as a campaign recommendation pending approval.
- Rebuild website hierarchy around event registration, mission proof and distinct secondary actions.
- Replace stale profile details, placeholder content and conflicting commerce routes.
- Build repeatable event, sponsor, impact, email and social templates.

## Rollout

**Phase 1 — approve:** leadership approves the strategy, message, tagline decision and logo-redraw brief. Resolve event pricing, beneficiary facts and commerce ownership.

**Phase 2 — produce:** create logo masters, templates, website components and a verified content library. Test contrast, mobile behavior and registration flow.

**Phase 3 — launch:** update website, Facebook, email signatures, HCUnits presentation, sponsor materials and store touchpoints in a coordinated release. Redirect or clearly retire stale routes.

**Phase 4 — govern:** assign an owner, maintain a source-of-truth event sheet and review links, prices, profile details and impact claims quarterly.

## Success measures

Track completed paid registrations, registration-page conversion, sponsor inquiries, donation completion, email growth and the share of public profiles with current details. The client goal is at least 80 November registrations and doubled website traffic; define the comparison period before reporting the latter.

## Risks

Launching new creative before operational facts are reconciled would amplify confusion. Treat medical history, memorial content and veteran stories with explicit approval. Keep new visual work nonpartisan and avoid implying military or beneficiary endorsement.
