# Red White and Clix — Brand Positioning

## Relationship to the primary benchmark

Stack Up validates the broader idea that gaming can create veteran connection and support. RWC's position must remain more specific: a veteran-founded, organizer-led tabletop event community centered on HeroClix and Midwest participation. Stack Up is the model for credibility and program clarity; RWC's differentiation is format, geography and the in-person event experience.

## 01 — Category definition

**Stated category:** Veteran-support nonprofit that runs tabletop events.  
**Competitive set:** Veteran-serving organizations that compete for attention, donors, volunteers, sponsors or participation. Stack Up is the closest competitor and primary benchmark; Operation Gratitude and Wounded Warrior Project are broader competitors.  
**Opportunity category:** **Veteran-founded tabletop event community.** This keeps the event experience and recurring belonging at the center while establishing the purpose.

Recommendation: use “veteran-founded tabletop event community” externally and “event-led veteran-support nonprofit” in formal organizational contexts.

## 02 — Landscape map

**Axis 1:** donation/service-led ←→ participation/play-led  
**Axis 2:** national/institutional ←→ local/human

- Wounded Warrior Project: national, service-led.
- Operation Gratitude: national, participatory volunteer action.
- Stack Up: national, gamer-native veteran support.
- RWC: local/human and play-led, with a veteran-founded mission.

The open territory is a serious regional tabletop experience where belonging and veteran support are inseparable but neither overwhelms the other.

## 03 — Territory

**The space:** The purposeful table—competition, camaraderie, and veteran support in one shared experience.

**Audience owned:** Midwest tabletop players and community partners who want participation to carry meaning.

**Moat:** Wesley’s lived context, organizer relationships, event history, local sponsor network, and the recurring physical community cannot be replicated by a generic donation campaign. The moat grows through operational quality and transparent impact, not merely the patriotic name.

## 04 — Statements

**Framework:** For tabletop gamers, veterans, and supporters, Red White and Clix is the veteran-founded event community that turns competitive play into camaraderie and support for veteran causes, because it combines lived experience, organized events, and an inclusive table.

**Strategic:** RWC owns the intersection of credible tabletop competition, Midwest community, and accountable veteran support.

**Human:** Come for a great game, find your people, and help the community stand with veterans.

## 05 — Proof points

1. Public event history and current November 7–8, 2026 HCUnits circuit.
2. Real event photography showing competitive play, prize moments, and community.
3. Veteran-founded story and public resource guide.
4. IRS record confirming 501(c)(3) status and deductible-contribution classification.
5. Sponsor and community relationships shown on the site; active relationships and permissions must be reconfirmed.

## 06 — Refusals

- RWC is not a partisan veterans organization.
- RWC is not a clinical, housing, crisis-response, or benefits provider.
- RWC will not use trauma or military service as spectacle.
- RWC will not trade event clarity for hype or publish impact it cannot substantiate.

## 07 — One sentence

Red White and Clix is the Midwest’s veteran-founded tabletop event community, bringing people together for credible competition, real camaraderie, and accountable support.

## Red flags to manage

“Community” and “support veterans” are generic without event-specific proof. “Combat veteran suicide through gaming” would overstate RWC’s documented role. The audience must remain segmented; “everyone” is not an actionable target.
