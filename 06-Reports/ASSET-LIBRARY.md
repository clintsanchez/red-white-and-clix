## Use notes

- Client-uploaded logo remains the primary identity source in `brand/` and pCloud `01-Brand-Assets/`.
- Every manifest records original source URL/page, dimensions, bytes and SHA-256. Signed Facebook CDN links can expire; the local files are preserved.
- Some site founder/archive images are only 206×206; do not enlarge them for hero use.
- Generated images, third-party logos, stock and merch graphics are reference assets, not evidence of an event.
- Selected photographs retain original pixels; no AI edits, cropping or enhancement applied.
- Human subjects and event dates are not identified solely from images. Confirm context and permissions before public reuse.
