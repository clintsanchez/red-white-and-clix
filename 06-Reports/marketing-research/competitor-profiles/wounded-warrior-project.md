## At a glance

Wounded Warrior Project (WWP) is a national veterans service organization focused on post-9/11 wounded, ill and injured veterans, families and caregivers. It provides programs, advocates and funds partner organizations.

## Positioning and messaging

**Value proposition:** Help wounded warriors and families thrive through comprehensive, no-cost services.  
**Audience:** Veterans and families seeking programs; donors; corporate and peer-to-peer fundraisers.  
**Angle:** Scale, comprehensive support, advocacy and lifelong impact.

## Offer and proof

WWP reports more than 270,000 registered warriors, family members and caregivers since 2003 and more than $260 million invested in programs and services in FY2024. Its annual reporting links claims to its Form 990.

## Strengths

- Very high national recognition and institutional proof.
- Broad service portfolio and no-cost participant programs.
- Mature impact reporting and fundraising infrastructure.
- Strong advocacy and partner network.

## Weaknesses and gaps

- Scale and broad scope reduce local intimacy.
- Gaming is not the core participation mechanism.
- Institutional messaging may feel distant from a hobby community.

## Implications for RWC

RWC should never compete on scale or imply comparable direct services. Its advantage is a specific, local experience and accessible founder/community relationship. Use precise language that distinguishes fundraising and awareness from direct veteran care.
