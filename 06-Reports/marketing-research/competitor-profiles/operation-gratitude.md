## At a glance

Operation Gratitude mobilizes volunteers, donors and partners to express appreciation to service members, veterans, first responders and military families through care packages, letters and hands-on service.

## Positioning and messaging

**Value proposition:** Make gratitude tangible through accessible acts of service.  
**Audience:** Individual volunteers, families, schools, companies and donors.  
**Angle:** Anyone can participate through time, supplies, letters or money.

## Offer and proof

The organization provides clearly defined recipient groups and volunteer actions. Its financial page reports that nearly 85% of financial contributions go to program services and links impact reporting and third-party transparency indicators.

## Strengths

- Tangible output makes participation easy to understand.
- Broad volunteer accessibility.
- Strong corporate and group fit.
- Clear financial and impact presentation.

## Weaknesses and gaps

- No gaming or competitive-event specialization.
- Gratitude/care-package model differs from ongoing gaming community.
- National program has less Lafayette-specific identity.

## Implications for RWC

RWC should make its impact as concrete as a care package: identify the beneficiary, action, amount or result, and reporting date. It can offer sponsors and families hands-on roles tied directly to the event.
