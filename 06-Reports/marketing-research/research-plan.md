# Red White and Clix — primary research plan

## Objective

Identify what drives registration, return attendance, sponsorship and trust before final campaign and website decisions.

## Sample

- 5–8 past attendees, including competitive and casual/newer players.
- 3 people who considered but did not attend.
- 3 veterans or military-family participants.
- 3 current or former sponsors.
- 2 representatives from supported organizations.

Recruit from registration records, email, Facebook and organizer referrals. Keep cohorts separate during analysis.

## Interview guide

1. Tell me about the last time you decided to attend—or skip—a HeroClix event.
2. What first put Red White and Clix on your radar?
3. What mattered most when you made the decision?
4. What nearly stopped you?
5. What did you expect the experience to be like?
6. What happened that you still remember?
7. How did the veteran mission affect your decision, if at all?
8. What was unclear before or after the event?
9. What would make you return or recommend it?
10. Who else should we talk to?

Ask “why?” and “tell me about a specific time” rather than seeking agreement with proposed messaging.

## Registration survey

Keep required questions minimal: first-time/returning, discovery source, travel distance band and primary reason for attending. After the event, ask satisfaction, likelihood to return, most valuable part, main friction and one open-ended improvement question.

## Analysis

Extract jobs, pains, triggers, outcomes, alternatives and exact language. Score themes by frequency and intensity; require at least five consistent data points before promoting a segment hypothesis to a persona conclusion.
