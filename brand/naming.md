# Red White and Clix — naming decision and system

## Decision

Keep **Red White and Clix** as the organization name. It connects patriotic color language with HeroClix, has legal and event-history equity, and is distinctive within the local tabletop category. A rename would add cost and recognition risk without solving the current operational issues.

## Standard form

- Display and legal name: **Red White and Clix**
- Short form after first reference: **RWC**
- Domain: **redwhiteandclix.org**
- Spoken form: “red, white and clicks”

Do not alternate among ampersands, commas or “Clixs.” Existing social display variants should be standardized where platform access permits. Use the exact legal form on receipts, filings and contracts.

## Naming risks

“Clix” may be unclear outside tabletop gaming, and “red, white and blue” associations can be mistaken for partisan positioning. Pair the name with a plain descriptor such as **Veteran-founded tabletop events**. Keep brand language explicitly nonpartisan and welcoming.

## Offer naming pattern

Use a branded-house system: `Red White and Clix + descriptive name`.

- Red White and Clix Annual Weekend
- RWC 300 Modern
- RWC Team Sealed
- RWC Veteran Resource Guide
- RWC Partner Program

Names should explain the offer, survive year-to-year changes and avoid unsupported words such as “national,” “championship” or “official.” Include the year only when it helps distinguish event editions.

## Governance

New program names require an audience fit check, trademark search, domain/social review when public-facing, and final approval from the executive director. This document records an existing-name decision; no new master-brand candidate was selected, so availability screening was not applicable.
