# Red White and Clix — identity system

## Direction

Preserve the recognizable red, white and blue wordmark while commissioning a clean vector redraw. The system should feel like an organized tabletop event with a public-service purpose: bold, welcoming and practical. The battlefield-cross detail must remain respectful and should never become decoration.

## Logo system

Required production set: primary stacked mark, horizontal lockup, one-color mark, simplified small icon and favicon. Use the full mark at 120 px or larger and clear space equal to 10% of its width. Never stretch, recolor, outline, shadow, crop, rotate or place it over a busy image. Until vector masters are approved, `red-white-and-clix-logo.png` is the authority.

## Color

| Role | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Logo red | `#FF0000` | Existing mark only; large accents |
| Logo blue | `#0072FF` | Existing mark only; large accents |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Space and reversed applications |
| Black | `#000000` | Existing mark |
| Action red | `#B91C1C` | Accessible buttons with white text |
| Link blue | `#0050B3` | Accessible links with white text |
| Ink | `#111827` | Body copy |
| Surface | `#F5F7FA` | Page backgrounds |

Logo red and blue do not meet 4.5:1 against white for normal text. Use the darker action colors for UI copy and controls.

## Typography

Use Archivo Black for short display headings and Space Grotesk for body, navigation and interface copy. Keep headings compact and body text generous: 16–18 px minimum, 1.6 line height. Use Arial as the fallback. Do not recreate the wordmark with type.

## Image direction

Prioritize real tables, players interacting, organizers working, group moments, prizes and the supported nonprofit in context. Favor natural color, eye-level viewpoints and visible participation. Label archival photographs. Treat generated art and product mockups as illustration, never evidence. Obtain permission before identifying veterans, health conditions or beneficiaries.

## Layout and graphic language

Use strong headlines, clean grids, generous white space, subtle game-grid geometry and direct event information. Red should signal action; blue should organize and reassure. Avoid military camouflage, distressed patriotic clichés, excessive flags, explosions and imagery that reduces veterans to trauma.
