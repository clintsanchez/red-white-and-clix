# Red White and Clix — brand audit

Audit date: September 21, 2026. Scope: intake, public website, Facebook, event listings, supplied assets and the new strategy package.

## Scorecard

| Dimension | Score / 5 | Finding |
|---|---:|---|
| Positioning | 3 | Authentic purpose is present; category language is inconsistent. |
| Visual identity | 3 | Recognizable mark and palette; production logo system is incomplete. |
| Messaging | 3 | Strong mission language; calls to action and impact claims need tighter governance. |
| Voice | 3 | Warm intent is clear; channel execution varies. |
| Audience fit | 3 | Core groups are known; newcomer and sponsor journeys need work. |
| Differentiation | 4 | Veteran-founded, participation-led tabletop focus is distinctive. |
| **Total** | **19 / 30** | Strong foundation with a significant implementation gap. |

## What is working

The founder story gives the mission credible roots. Real event photography shows active community. The name is memorable in the HeroClix category. Current event listings, nonprofit status and a supported charity provide useful proof once presented consistently.

## Priority gaps

1. Conflicting prices and purchase routes undermine registration confidence.
2. Placeholder donation answers weaken trust even though the federal exemption record is now verifiable.
3. Facebook carries an old domain and email.
4. Two merchandise catalogs create an unclear shopping path.
5. Vector, horizontal and small-format logo masters do not exist.
6. Generated art and mockups need clear separation from documentary photography.
7. Impact totals, beneficiary terms and sponsor permissions need a documented approval source.

## Brand-to-experience gap

The new brand package defines a focused, transparent organization. The public experience still contains stale links, conflicting commerce details and incomplete proof. The immediate brand task is implementation: make every event, donation and contact detail agree across channels.

## Actions

**First 30 days:** reconcile registration prices and links; replace donation placeholders; update Facebook; choose one primary shop; approve message spine and campaign line.

**30–60 days:** produce vector logo suite; implement accessible UI colors and type roles; rebuild homepage and event path; document sponsor/beneficiary permissions.

**60–90 days:** launch consistent templates; publish a simple impact report; establish quarterly channel audits and event conversion reporting.
