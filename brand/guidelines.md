# Red White and Clix — brand guidelines

Version 2, September 21, 2026. This guide is the working source of truth. Existing assets are labeled established; strategic and creative recommendations require client approval.

## 1. Brand foundation

**Mission:** Bring people together through well-run tabletop gaming events that create camaraderie, raise awareness and support veteran-focused causes.

**Vision:** Become the Midwest’s most trusted veteran-founded tabletop event community.

**Values:** belonging; play with purpose; service without spectacle; transparent impact; camaraderie and fair competition.

**Positioning:** Red White and Clix is the veteran-founded tabletop event community for players and supporters who want serious play, genuine connection and a clear way to support veteran causes.

**Brand promise:** A welcoming seat, a well-run event and an honest account of the mission your participation supports.

**Architecture:** Use one branded house. Programs follow `RWC + descriptive name`; see `architecture.md`.

## 2. Identity

The supplied `red-white-and-clix-logo.png` is the current mark. Preserve its proportions and use a dark backing when required. Minimum proposed size is 120 CSS px; clear space is 10% of mark width. Never stretch, crop, recolor, shadow, rotate or place it over a busy image. Commission stacked, horizontal, one-color, small-icon and favicon vector masters.

| Role | Hex | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Logo red | `#FF0000` | Established from mark |
| Logo blue | `#0072FF` | Established from mark |
| White / black | `#FFFFFF` / `#000000` | Established from mark |
| Action red | `#B91C1C` | Recommended accessible UI |
| Link blue | `#0050B3` | Recommended accessible UI |
| Ink / surface | `#111827` / `#F5F7FA` | Recommended UI |

Use Archivo Black for display headings and Space Grotesk for copy and interface text, with Arial fallback. Lead with authentic event imagery: people playing, connecting and organizing. Keep archival, generated and product imagery clearly labeled. Avoid camouflage, distressed patriotic clichés and trauma-centered veteran imagery. Full details: `identity.md`.

## 3. Voice

Sound like an organized host: welcoming, grounded, energetic, respectful and practical. Lead event content with what, when, where and how. Explain insider terms. Use active voice and a single clear call to action.

Say: play, join, welcome, community, camaraderie, participate, veterans, support, sponsor, volunteer, together.

Avoid partisan language, pity, combat metaphors for ordinary marketing, medical promises, official endorsement implications, fabricated urgency and unsupported impact claims. Full rules and examples: `voice.md`.

## 4. Messaging

**Core message:** Tabletop play creates a place to belong and a practical way to stand with veterans.

**Value proposition:** Well-run tabletop events where competition, camaraderie and veteran support share the same table.

**Recommended campaign line:** **Play Together. Stand With Veterans.** The established “One Community. One Mission.” may remain the organizational sign-off until the client approves a change.

Message pillars are a seat at the table, competition with camaraderie, understandable purpose, and veteran-founded local roots. Support claims with verified event facts, real photography, IRS records, documented beneficiary relationships and approved testimonials. CTA priority: Register, Donate, Sponsor, Volunteer. Full system: `messaging.md`.

## 5. Applications

**Website:** Make registration the primary path. Show current date, place, format, fee and authoritative purchase link together. Keep mission proof visible and donation, sponsor and shop actions distinct.

**Social:** Put date and location early, use real images, link directly to the action and keep profile contact details current.

**Email:** One useful update, one main action and plain-text contact details.

**Sponsor materials:** Use verified audience and deliverables. Obtain logo permissions and report fulfillment.

**Print:** Do not enlarge the raster logo for large production; obtain vector masters and a print proof first.

Before publication, confirm dates, fees, URLs, beneficiary language, photo permissions, contrast, mobile readability and claim evidence.
