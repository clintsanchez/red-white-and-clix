# Website brief — working scope

Prepared September 21, 2026 from the completed intake, brand system and repository research. This is a reviewable planning brief; live changes have not been made.

## Outcome

Give visitors a clear route from discovering Red White and Clix to understanding the next event and completing the correct registration. Make the veteran mission and beneficiary relationship understandable. Make routine updates manageable for the owner.

## Decisions still open

Platform direction, implementation budget, delivery date, decision-maker for site sign-off and long-term maintenance owner. Squarespace is the recorded current platform, not an agreed future-platform decision. This brief remains platform-neutral until that choice is supplied.

## First release: four core pages and the registration journey

| Page | Main job | Required sections | Completion condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | Explain the organization and point to the event | Mission-led introduction; upcoming event; participation routes; real photography; current contact | One dominant event action, understandable mission, no unsupported claims |
| Events | Be the authoritative event-information hub | Date and venue; formats; fee and basis; schedule; access/travel; registration route; contact | Event facts match the chosen checkout and organizer-approved details |
| About | Establish who organizes the community and why | Approved founder story; mission; evidence-backed organization details; contact | Biography and claims reviewed, no unapproved personal detail |
| Donate | Explain what giving supports | Beneficiary relationship; allocation; donation route; receipt/FAQ answers | Every material giving statement has an owner and supporting record |

Keep existing working merchandise and resource paths accessible during the first release. Expand them after the primary event journey is accurate. A separate impact page, sponsor hub and new-player guide are the next content priorities when evidence and ownership support them.

## Working navigation

Events · Our Mission · Veteran Resources · Support Us · Shop · About. This is a proposed information hierarchy, not a reason to create six new pages immediately. Support Us may group donation, sponsorship and volunteering, with one appropriate action on each destination.

## URL treatment

Current audit references `/`, `/home`, `/services-store`, `/about`, `/donate` and commerce pages. Preserve existing useful destinations until a route inventory and redirect plan are reviewed. A clean `/events` hub is a proposed destination; do not repoint a commerce or checkout URL solely to improve its name. Determine the platform's available URL/canonical controls during implementation. Confirm redirect behavior and purchase continuity before any URL changes.

## Event content model

One record per format: event name, date, local start/check-in times and timezone, venue/address, rules link, price, currency, fee basis, registration URL, refund terms, availability, organizer contact, last-verified date and factual owner. Team Sealed needs separate fields for teams purchased and individual participants.

## Visual implementation

Use the supplied primary identity, Archivo Black display, Space Grotesk body, darker accessible action colors and the `rwc-` tokens. Apply the component library to event cards, schedule, registration actions, donation allocation/FAQ, sponsor grid and resources. Nine new logo variants are review options; do not substitute a wide or single-color mark automatically.
