# Measurement and access handoff

Prepared September 21, 2026. This is the setup specification; no accounts or tracking have been configured.

## Define success before showing a dashboard

| Metric | Definition to settle | Current value | Source needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Traffic | Choose sessions or users; use one comparable reporting window and timezone | Unknown | Existing analytics export and baseline dates |
| Paid people | Unique people with valid paid registration; exclude cancelled/refunded entries under the agreed rule | Unknown | Registration records with participant/team structure |
| Tournament entries | Valid entries by format; a person may enter more than one event | Unknown | Event-level registration export |
| Team purchases | Paid Team Sealed purchases, distinct from individuals | Unknown | Team checkout records and roster/count confirmation |
| Registration intent | Clicks to the chosen registration destination | Not configured/verified | Site event tracking |
| Donation outcomes | Confirmed completed gifts, separate from outbound donation clicks | Unknown | Donation-platform records |

“Double traffic” needs a named baseline and equivalent comparison window. “80 registrations” needs the client's definition. Do not put zero in the starting cells or assume a team purchase equals three registered individuals until the roster/counting policy is known.

## Proposed reporting approach

Capture event-page visits and registration outbound clicks separately. Record event/format, originating page and campaign source where available. Completed payment must come from a confirmed checkout completion mechanism or reconciled registration export. An outbound click does not demonstrate a sale. If the registration platform cannot provide conversion integration, use periodic owner-supplied reports and clearly label attribution limits.

Review weekly during the event campaign. Report baseline, current period, change, source date and data gaps. Exclude identifiable participant data from the general marketing dashboard. Keep aggregate registration and donation totals separate from private rosters and payment records.

## Access checklist

| System | Needed for | What to record |
|---|---|---|
| Website platform | Content/layout changes and URL review | Account owner; delegated collaborator; edit/publish permissions |
| Domain/DNS | Only if the chosen scope requires domain changes | Owner and authorized administrator; no password in this checklist |
| Registration platform | Correct checkout route and completed registration reporting | Organizer account owner; available exports/integrations |
| Analytics/Search Console | Baseline, discovery and measurement | Existing property identifiers, owner and delegated access |
| Facebook | Correcting the outdated domain/contact fields | Page owner and delegated editing role |
| Donation/merchandise providers | Accurate routes, receipts and fulfillment | Provider, owner and confirmed responsibilities |

Use delegated access/invitations through the provider or the agency's established secure-sharing process. Do not paste passwords into planning documents. The old handoff's mention of a credential file does not establish that access works on this machine.

## Maintenance handoff

Assign a primary owner and backup for event facts, fee/availability changes, donor transparency, sponsor permissions and routine site edits. Define who publishes and who verifies each update. Record the actual person after confirmation; agency/client role suggestions are not staffing commitments.
