# Red White and Clix — brand context

Source: Wesley Robertson's GHL submission of September 16 UTC (September 15 Central), official website and September 21 research. See `../06-Reports/organization-research.md` for evidence and conflicts.

Veteran-founded tabletop gaming events for gamers, veterans, military families and community supporters, rooted in Lafayette, Indiana and serving a Midwest audience. The differentiator is active participation and camaraderie through games rather than a donation-only appeal. Primary conversion: November event registration. Secondary conversions: donations, sponsorship, volunteer interest and merchandise.

Client goals: double traffic, at least 80 November registrations, secondary-event details and finances established within six months. Voice: professional, inspirational, empathetic/warm. Explicit constraint: no politics or divisive content; everyone should feel welcome.

The supplied logo is the identity authority. Exact sampled opaque colors: red #FF0000, white #FFFFFF, blue #0072FF, black #000000. The live website loads Archivo Black and Space Grotesk. No formal guide was supplied. Logo variants, body font weights and new UI treatments need to be described as proposals, not prior approvals.

Values inferred from client language: inclusion, camaraderie, service, practical support and fair competition. Proposed positioning: a welcoming tabletop event community where playing together supports veteran causes.
