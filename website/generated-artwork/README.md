# Generated artwork — NOT photography

Everything in this folder is AI-generated artwork created for the RWC website.
Per the project brief it must never be labeled, captioned, or published as a
photograph of a real event, a real person, or a real product.

## Hero figure concepts — September 22, 2026

Original character designs for the website hero. They are deliberately NOT
HeroClix, ActionClix, Marvel, or DC figures: those are WizKids/NECA brands
under Marvel and DC license, and a 501(c)(3) cannot use them as brand
furniture. These are original works the client would own outright.

- `rwc-figure-concept-a.png` — male figure, planted heroic stance, 16:9
- `rwc-figure-concept-b.png` — female figure, open-palm welcoming gesture, 16:9

Both are composed with the figure in the right third and the left half held as
near-black negative space, to sit behind the Aloohxi-style hero headline.

Engine: Gemini 3 Pro Image (`nanobanana` MCP). Prompts are recorded in the
session transcript.

Brand constraints honored: no camouflage, no combat imagery, no distressed
patriotic cliche, no trauma-centered veteran imagery, no existing character
likeness, no text or insignia on the figure.

Open item: the client (brand guardian Wesley Robertson) approves the final
character before it becomes a brand asset.
