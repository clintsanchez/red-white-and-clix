OUR STORY / RED WHITE AND CLIX

Veteran-founded.
Community-powered.

A shared hobby. A welcoming table. A practical way to stand with veterans.

WHY WE GATHER

Connection begins
with taking part.

Wesley Robertson created Red White and Clix to connect the camaraderie of tabletop gaming with a practical commitment to veteran support.

The community brings players, veterans, families and supporters together through organized events rooted in Indiana and open to the wider Midwest.

Serious play and a welcoming atmosphere belong at the same table.

OUR MISSION

Bring people together through well-run tabletop gaming events that create camaraderie, raise awareness and support veteran-focused causes.

WHAT GUIDES US

A community you
can participate in.

01 / BELONGING

A welcoming seat.

Newcomers, regular players, veterans and families all have a place in the community.

02 / FAIR PLAY

Competition with respect.

The experience should be organized, enjoyable and grounded in good sportsmanship.

03 / TRANSPARENCY

Purpose you can understand.

Support should be explained through confirmed relationships, clear allocation and documented results.
