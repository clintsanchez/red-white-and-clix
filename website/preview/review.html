INTERNAL REVIEW / NOT A LIVE SITE

Four pages.
One clear journey.

Review the visual hierarchy, wording and movement from the homepage to the event details. This static preview leaves platform choice open.

What works here

  • Four linked pages: home, events, our story and giving.
  • Responsive desktop/mobile layouts and navigation.
  • Event section links, email/phone links and native FAQ disclosures.
  • Local fonts, the supplied primary logo and documentary photo references.

What still needs a decision

  • Platform, implementation scope/budget, timing and maintenance owner.
  • Event fees and fee basis, checkout links, check-in/start times and timezone, rules, refunds, availability and venue access.
  • Beneficiary relationships, allocation, donation recipient and receipts.
  • Photo context/permission and final content sign-off.
  • Traffic baseline, definition of the 80-registration goal and completed-payment reporting.
Sources and limitations

Dates, venue and contact details are carried from the repository’s September 21, 2026 research. They were not reverified live during this preview build. Registration and donation controls are intentionally disabled until authoritative destinations are supplied.

Resource and shop links lead to the existing external website. This preview makes no production account changes, collects no form/payment information and contains no analytics integration.

Review sequence

  1. Start on Home, then open each event card.
  2. Check whether the event information answers the questions needed to register.
  3. Read Our story and the giving-page structure.
  4. Try the mobile menu and FAQ disclosures at phone width.
  5. Record feedback against the appropriate page before implementation.
