## Who we are

Red, White, and Clix is a veteran-founded nonprofit running tabletop gaming
events. We decide what information this website collects and why, which makes
us the data controller for it.

Questions about this policy, or about information we hold about you, go to
**redwhiteandclix@gmail.com** or **(574) 265-9585**. A real person answers, and
we would rather you asked than wondered.

## What we collect

### When you just read the site

Nothing that identifies you personally, and nothing at all until you agree to it.

We use **Google Analytics 4** to count visits and see which pages help people
find an event and register. It is switched off when the page loads. It starts
only if you choose **Accept** on the cookie banner, and if you choose **Decline**
it never runs at all — no cookies are set and nothing is sent to Google.

If you accept, Google Analytics collects:

- which pages you viewed, in what order, and how long you stayed
- the site or search engine that sent you here
- your device type, browser, screen size and language
- an approximate location, no more precise than city level

Your IP address is used momentarily to work out that approximate location and is
**not stored** by Google Analytics. We have turned off Google Signals and
advertising personalisation, so this data is not used to build an advertising
profile of you or to follow you across devices. We do not upload email addresses
or any other identifier to Google.

We keep this analytics data for **14 months**, after which Google deletes it
automatically.

### When you fill in the contact form

We collect only what you type: your **name**, your **email address**, a **phone
number if you choose to give one**, and **whatever you write in the message** —
for example which days you can volunteer or which format you want to play.

We use it to reply to you and to follow up about the event you asked about.
That is all. We do not need it for anything else and we do not go looking for
more.

Submissions are sent to and stored in **GoHighLevel**, a customer relationship
tool operated by HighLevel, Inc. in the United States, which our web agency
administers on our behalf. Your message is sent to them from our server; their
software does not run in your browser and sets no cookies on this site.

We keep a submission for as long as we are still dealing with you and for
**two years** after our last contact, so that we recognise you if you come back
to a later event. Then we delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner and we
will.

**We will not add you to a mailing list because you used the contact form.** If
we ever start sending event announcements, we will ask you to opt in separately,
and every message will carry a one-click unsubscribe.

### Server logs

Our host, **Railway**, keeps standard web server logs — IP address, time, page
requested, browser — for a short period. These exist to keep the site up and to
spot abuse. They are not used to analyse visitors and are not combined with
anything else. We do not use them for marketing.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Ever. Not to anyone, not
  for any amount.
- We do not run advertising pixels, remarketing tags or social media trackers.
- We do not record your screen or your mouse movements.
- We do not ask for information we have no use for.
- We never see your card or bank details. Donations and purchases happen on
  other companies' systems, described below.

## Cookies

Full detail is on our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy). In short: this site sets
**no cookies unless you accept them**, the only cookies it ever sets are Google
Analytics ones, and you can change your mind at any time.

## Other companies, and when you leave this site

We do not embed anything from other companies. Everything you see on a page —
fonts, images, styles, scripts — is served from our own domain. But several
things we do happen on someone else's website, and once you arrive there you are
under their privacy policy, not ours:

| What | Who runs it | What happens there |
|---|---|---|
| Registering for an event | HCUnits (hcunits.net) | You create or use an account with them |
| Donating | PayPal and Venmo | They take the payment and see the card details; we never do |
| Buying merchandise | Printify | A separate store with its own account, checkout and cookies |
| Our Facebook page | Meta | Standard Facebook tracking applies once you are on their site |
| Government and veterans services | The VA and others | We link out so you can reach them directly |

We have no control over and no visibility of what those sites collect. We are
not paid by any of them for sending you there.

## Your choices and your rights

You can ask us to:

- **tell you** what we hold about you
- **correct it** if it is wrong
- **delete it** — and we will, unless we are legally required to keep something
- **stop using it** for follow-up

Email **redwhiteandclix@gmail.com** and we will act on it. We will not make you
prove a legal right before we help you, and we will not charge you.

You can also refuse or withdraw analytics consent at any time using the cookie
banner or by clearing this site's cookies in your browser.

If you are in the UK, EU or a US state with its own privacy law, you may have
additional statutory rights, including the right to complain to your regulator.
We will honour those requests regardless of where you live, because drawing a
line by geography would be a strange way to treat people who support veterans.

## Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
information from them. If you believe a child has sent us something, tell us and
we will delete it. Young people are welcome at our events with a parent or
guardian; that is arranged in person, not through this website.

## Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Contact submissions travel encrypted to
GoHighLevel. Access to that account is limited to the people who need it. We are
a small organisation and we will not pretend to guarantee perfect security, but
we do not keep information we do not need, which is the most reliable protection
there is.

## Changes to this policy

If we start collecting something new, add a service, or change who we share with,
we will update this page and change the date at the top **before** that happens,
not after. That is how this page came to be rewritten: analytics and the contact
form were decided on, so the policy changed first.
