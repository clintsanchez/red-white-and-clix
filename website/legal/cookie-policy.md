## The short version

This site sets **no cookies until you say yes**. The only cookies it ever sets
are two Google Analytics cookies that count visits. Decline and none are set.
There is nothing else — no advertising, no social widgets, no tracking pixels.

## Why there is a banner now

There did not used to be one, because the site set no cookies at all and there
was nothing to consent to. That has changed: we want to know whether the work
we are doing actually brings more people to the event, and that means counting
visitors.

We could have added analytics quietly. Plenty of sites do. Instead the rule we
have kept to is that this page changes **before** the cookies appear, not after.

## What we set, if you accept

| Cookie | Set by | Lasts | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | 13 months | Tells one returning browser from another, so two visits from you are not counted as two people |
| `_ga_<ID>` | Google Analytics | 13 months | Keeps track of a single visit while you move between pages |

Both are **first-party** cookies — they are stored against redwhiteandclix.org,
not against Google. Neither contains your name, your email address or anything
you typed. Neither is readable by another website.

We have turned off Google Signals and advertising personalisation, so these
cookies are not used to build an advertising profile or to follow you onto other
sites. What we get is a count of visits and a list of which pages people read.

## What we do not set

- No advertising or remarketing cookies.
- No social media cookies. There is no Facebook pixel, no Like button, no
  embedded feed. Our Facebook page is a plain link.
- No session recording or heatmap cookies. We do not watch your mouse.
- No cookies from any other company. Fonts, images, styles and scripts all come
  from our own domain, so no third party learns you visited by you simply
  loading a page.

## Saying no, and changing your mind

**Decline** on the banner and no analytics cookies are set and no data is sent to
Google. The site works exactly the same — nothing is withheld, nothing nags you,
and we do not ask again on every page.

To change your answer later, clear this site's cookies in your browser and the
banner will ask again. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies from
its Privacy settings, and most offer a "block third-party cookies" switch that
this site will never trip, because it does not use any.

Your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage rather than in a
cookie, so recording a "no" does not itself set the thing you said no to.

## Other sites you reach from here

When you follow a link to register for an event, donate, buy merchandise, or
reach a government service, you leave this site. Those companies set their own
cookies under their own policies, and we have no control over or visibility of
what they do:

- **HCUnits** — event registration
- **PayPal** and **Venmo** — donations
- **Printify** — the merchandise store
- **Facebook** — our page
- **The VA** and other veterans organizations — the services we link to

## What would change this page

If we ever add an embedded map, embedded video, a social feed, a chat widget or
an on-site payment step, each would bring cookies or third-party requests with
it. As before: this page gets updated and the banner gets adjusted **first**.
