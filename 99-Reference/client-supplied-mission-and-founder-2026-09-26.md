## Vision Statement

A world where veterans and the communities around them are connected through
shared experiences, inclusive engagement, and meaningful support, ensuring
veterans are seen, valued, and never isolated or forgotten.

## Mission Statement

Red White and Clix bridges veterans and their communities through open and
inclusive tabletop gaming events that create meaningful connection, raise
awareness of veteran needs, and generate community-supported funding for
veteran-focused causes through shared experiences and fun.

## Who We Are

Red White and Clix Inc. is a veteran-led 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization that
brings veterans and communities together through tabletop gaming, shared
experiences, and community engagement.

Founded by disabled veteran Wesley Robertson, Red White and Clix was inspired by
firsthand understanding of the invisible struggles many veterans face after
returning home. Feelings of isolation, loss of connection, and uncertainty can
continue long after military service ends, often leaving veterans unsure where
to turn or whether support truly exists for them.

Rather than focusing solely on those struggles, Red White and Clix focuses on
building connection, hope, and community through positive and engaging
experiences that naturally bring people together.

At its core, the organization is built on the belief that fun, camaraderie, and
shared passions can become powerful tools for meaningful impact.

## One Community. One Mission.

"One Community. One Mission." reflects the belief that supporting veterans is
not the responsibility of one group, organization, or generation alone. It is
something that communities can unite around together.

Red White and Clix exists to bring people together through shared experiences,
fun, and inclusive engagement while creating meaningful support for veterans and
raising awareness of the challenges many still face after service. No matter our
backgrounds, we are one community with a common mission: ensuring veterans are
seen, supported, and never forgotten.

## How We Create Impact

Red White and Clix hosts open and inclusive community gaming events centered
around tabletop hobbies such as HeroClix, board games, card games, roleplaying
games, and other shared gaming experiences that naturally bring people together.

Events are designed to create welcoming spaces where veterans, civilians,
families, businesses, sponsors, and community members can connect through fun,
teamwork, conversation, and camaraderie while supporting veteran-focused causes.

Typical event formats include:

- Competitive 1v1 tournaments
- Team-based events
- Sealed and draft-style formats
- Free-for-all multiplayer experiences
- Special feature events and community activities

Through tournament entry fees, sponsorships, fundraising activities, raffles,
prizes, donations, and community partnerships, Red White and Clix generates
meaningful financial support for veteran-focused causes while strengthening
community connection and engagement.

## Why It Matters

Founder Wesley Robertson created Red White and Clix based on his own experiences
after serving in the U.S. Army. Like many veterans returning home, he struggled
with invisible wounds, isolation, and the feeling that he was alone in what he
was experiencing. Not knowing where to turn or whether meaningful support even
existed, he endured the best way he knew how on his own.

Countless veterans continue to face those same struggles every day.

Red White and Clix exists to help close the gap between veterans, the
communities around them, and the organizations already working to support them.
Through open and inclusive tabletop gaming events, veterans and civilians come
together through shared experiences, fun, and genuine human connection.

More than a gaming event, Red White and Clix gives veterans hope, community, and
the reminder that they are not alone, while giving communities an opportunity to
show appreciation and support through meaningful action rather than words alone.

## Founder Story

Wesley Robertson served as a Combat Engineer in the Indiana Army National Guard
from 1999 to 2006. In 2004, he deployed to Mosul, Iraq, operating out of Forward
Operating Base Marez in support of Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) operations.
His role placed him in close proximity to the daily realities of war, where the
risks were constant and the stakes were measured in lives.

Like many who served during this period, the challenges did not end when the
deployment ended. The transition home carried invisible wounds. Over time,
Wesley was diagnosed with severe Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) and later
Primary Progressive Multiple Sclerosis (MS). These diagnoses fundamentally
altered the trajectory of his life, creating barriers that impacted his health,
career, and sense of stability.

Compounding these struggles was the painful reality that several fellow service
members did not survive the years after returning home. Watching brothers-in-arms
lose their battles to suicide left a lasting impact and created a powerful sense
of responsibility to act. The realization that the war continued long after the
battlefield became a defining turning point.

During one of the most difficult seasons of his life, Wesley received support
from a veteran-focused charitable organization. That support provided not only
practical assistance but also a renewed sense of connection and hope. It
demonstrated firsthand how meaningful community support can be for veterans
navigating complex physical and mental health challenges.

Motivated by both loss and gratitude, Wesley founded Red White and Clix to
transform personal adversity into positive impact for others. The organization
was created to build community through tabletop gaming while raising funds and
awareness for veteran-focused causes.

The mission is simple but powerful: create welcoming spaces where connection
happens naturally, conversations are easier, and support can be generated in
ways that feel authentic rather than clinical.

Since its founding, Red White and Clix has demonstrated that community-centered
events can produce measurable support for veterans and their families. The
inaugural event raised over $15,000 for veteran-focused charities, proving that
shared passions can become engines for meaningful change.

Today, Red White and Clix continues to grow as a platform for connection,
purpose, and tangible impact. The organization exists not only to raise funds,
but to create visible reminders that veterans are not alone after service ends.

Every event reinforces the belief that community engagement can play a direct
role in reducing isolation, restoring purpose, and strengthening support networks
for those who have served.

Red White and Clix reflects a broader commitment to ensuring that the challenges
veterans face after service are met with compassion, innovation, and sustained
public support.
