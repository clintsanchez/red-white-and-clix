2026 CHARITY WE ARE SUPPORTING
Behind every game piece is a real story.
Many veterans carry invisible burdens long after their service ends. Transitioning to civilian life can mean navigating isolation, loss of purpose, physical injuries, PTSD, or financial hardship. These challenges don’t always show on the surface, but they shape daily life for countless men and women who have served.
Red White & Clix exists to turn community into action.
We bring people together through tabletop gaming events that create connection, purpose, and tangible support for veteran-focused causes. What may look like a game night on the outside is actually something deeper — a shared mission to raise funds, raise awareness, and build a community that stands behind those who have sacrificed for others.
As a disabled veteran, I’ve experienced firsthand how powerful community can be. Support doesn’t always come from grand gestures. Sometimes it comes from people showing up, participating, and choosing to be part of something meaningful.
Every sponsor, every player, and every supporter helps transform a hobby into a vehicle for impact.
Together, we are proving that even small communities can create real change.
Because supporting veterans shouldn't be a one-day gesture.
It should be an ongoing commitment.

300-Point Modern Championship
Build your team. Choose your map. Roll the dice. Change the battle.
HeroClix is a collectible miniatures game where superheroes, villains, monsters, and legends collide in tactical combat. Every character has a point value, and in
300 Modern
, players assemble a force totaling no more than 300 points using figures and game elements from the current Modern Age.
For new players, think of it as superhero chess with dice: move your characters across a detailed map, use their unique powers, and outmaneuver your opponent to score points. For experienced players, this is RWC ’26’s premier competitive event—a test of team construction, positioning, resource management, and knowing exactly when to unleash your biggest attack.
Players will compete in Swiss rounds based on attendance, with the highest-ranked
32 players advancing to the championship cut
. There is
no player cap
, so bring your best force and take your shot at becoming the RWC ’26 300-Point Modern Champion.
Prize details will be announced soon.

3v3 team sealed
Grab two teammates, crack open some boosters, and turn whatever fate hands you into a winning strategy.
In
Team Sealed
, each three-player team receives unopened HeroClix boosters and builds its forces using only the characters and game elements pulled at the event. That means no perfectly prepared collection and no guaranteed combinations—just teamwork, quick decisions, and the challenge of making the most of what you open.
For newer players, Team Sealed is a fun way to experience HeroClix without needing a large collection of your own. For veteran players, it tests a completely different set of skills: evaluating figures on the spot, dividing resources between teammates, building three effective forces, and adjusting your strategy as a group.
Each teammate will face a member of the opposing team, with the individual games combining to determine the winning team. Communicate, build smart, and battle together for the title of
RWC ’26 Team Sealed Champions
.
Prize details will be announced soon.

battle royale
Four players. Four boosters. One map. Absolute chaos.
A HeroClix
Battle Royale
is a fast-paced, four-player free-for-all where every player opens one sealed booster and heads straight into battle using the characters inside. Alliances may form, betrayals are inevitable, and the player leading the game can become everyone’s favorite target in a hurry.
For newer players, Battle Royales are an easy and exciting way to jump into HeroClix without bringing a prebuilt force or a large collection. For veteran players, they offer a completely different challenge—quickly evaluating unfamiliar figures, navigating a crowded battlefield, and knowing exactly when to attack, negotiate, or stay out of the crossfire.
After each game, all opened figures are placed into a prize pool along with a randomly selected Convention Exclusive figure. Players then choose their prizes through a snake draft based on final placement, so everyone leaves with something.
Battle Royale points will be tracked throughout the weekend. The player with the highest cumulative score will be crowned the
RWC ’26 Battle Royale Champion
and receive a championship trophy, a sealed HeroClix brick, and a Chase/Vault Booster.
