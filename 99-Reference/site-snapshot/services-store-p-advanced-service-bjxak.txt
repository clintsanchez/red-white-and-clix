Hot Clix
$199.00
sold out
Hot Clix is a 1v1 sealed format where each player builds a 300-point team from two booster packs provided at the event. At the start of each turn, players must eat a boneless hot wing (with a fork to keep hands and figures clean), with the heat level increasing throughout the game—there is no time limit, and play continues until one team is eliminated or a player taps out.
If a player takes a drink to cool down, they suffer a penalty by taking unavoidable damage to a figure of their opponent’s choosing, adding an extra layer of risk, endurance, and chaos to every match.
