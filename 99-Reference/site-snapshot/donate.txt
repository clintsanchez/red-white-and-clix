Why Give?
Make a Difference
For many veterans, the transition to civilian life can present significant and ongoing challenges. Financial hardship, mental health struggles, and loss of community connection are realities that continue long after service ends. Support systems matter. Connection matters. Community matters. Red White and Clix exists to create visible, tangible hope by transforming community engagement into charitable action. By bringing people together through shared experiences, the organization creates opportunities for individuals and businesses to directly contribute to meaningful change. Each event becomes more than a gathering. It becomes a statement that community can play a role in supporting those who have sacrificed in service to others. Red White and Clix demonstrates that even something as simple as sitting down at a table together can become the starting point for something meaningful
