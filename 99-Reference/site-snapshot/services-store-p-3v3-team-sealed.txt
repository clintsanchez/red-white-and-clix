3v3 Team Sealed is a cooperative format where teams of three players build their forces from a shared pool of sealed product, consisting of a 12-booster brick divided among teammates. Players construct their teams from this limited pool and compete in coordinated one-on-one matches, relying on strategy, teamwork, and communication to outscore opposing teams.
