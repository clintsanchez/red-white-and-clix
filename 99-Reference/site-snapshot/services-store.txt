300 Modern
Heroclix 300 Modern Age is the standard competitive format where players build a team of characters totaling up to 300 points using figures from the current Modern Age set pool. Players compete head-to-head, using strategy and character abilities to outmaneuver opponents and score points by KO’ing opposing figures within a set time limit.

3v3 Team Sealed
3v3 Team Sealed is a cooperative format where teams of three players build their forces from a shared pool of sealed product, consisting of a 12-booster brick divided among teammates. Players construct their teams from this limited pool and compete in coordinated one-on-one matches, relying on strategy, teamwork, and communication to outscore opposing teams.

Hot Clix
Hot Clix is a 1v1 sealed format where each player builds a 300-point team from two booster packs provided at the event. At the start of each turn, players must eat a boneless hot wing (with a fork to keep hands and figures clean), with the heat level increasing throughout the game—there is no time limit, and play continues until one team is eliminated or a player taps out.
If a player takes a drink to cool down, they suffer a penalty by taking unavoidable damage to a figure of their opponent’s choosing, adding an extra layer of risk, endurance, and chaos to every match.
